# Francisco Javier Chichón García

Senior Specialized Technical Officer at the Centro Nacional de Biotecnología (CNB-CSIC), Madrid.

## About

More than 20 years of applied experience in cryo-electron microscopy (cryo-EM), electron tomography (ET) and cryo-soft X-ray tomography (cSXT), with a focus on quantitative 3D information extraction, image quality improvement and process automation.

- Commissioning, operation and optimization of advanced electron and X-ray microscopy instrumentation.
- Sample preparation, data acquisition, quantitative analysis and 3D model generation.
- Development and maintenance of software, scripts and scientific workflows.
- User training and scientific-technical support in international consortia (BioStructX, iNEXT, INSTRUCT-ERIC).
- Supervision of PhD and Master's theses.
- Teaching and training activity since 2009 in cryo-microscopy, biological tomography and correlative microscopy.
